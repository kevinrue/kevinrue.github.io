Why I Read This Paper

This paper caught my attention because I regularly process single-cell transcriptomics data for various projects. I was particularly curious about the method’s capacity to model multiple conditions and time course experiments, and how it might apply to one of my current datasets.

We read this paper as part of our group’s journal club, focusing this time on single-cell analysis methods.
